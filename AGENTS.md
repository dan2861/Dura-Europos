# Working instructions

## Collaboration
- Guide me through the work step by step. Explain the purpose of each step and ask for clarification when needed.
- Let me perform tasks unless they explicitly request execution. Authorization applies to the requested scope.
- Inspect external sources read-only by default. Do not edit Wikidata, shared documents, or spreadsheets without an explicit request.
- Do not send messages to colleagues without explicit authorization.

## Evidence and data
- Distinguish directly observed evidence, findings carried forward from discussion, inferences, and unresolved questions.
- Preserve identifiers exactly, including apostrophes, tildes, punctuation, and leading zeros.
- Keep original files in sources/ unchanged. Store working reviews in review/ and proposed updates in changes/.
- A photograph of a plan can correctly describe an archival photograph whose subject is a plan. Check the entity being described.
- An IIIF manifest or image URL is not automatically a suitable P973 record destination.
- Do not interpret a retired Artstor service as a deprecated Wikidata statement rank.

## Project continuity
- Read SUMMARY.md before substantive work and update it after material findings or decisions.
- Keep sample evidence in review/sample-review.csv; do not replace uncertainties with unsupported conclusions.
- Before external updates, preserve the starting data and prepare a concrete reviewable change set.
- Record export scope, method, timestamps, and limitations with backups. Do not label a partial export complete.
